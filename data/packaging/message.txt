Opening Highwater starts its daemon through D-Bus activation. The desktop session also
starts Highwater hidden when a StatusNotifier host is available, keeping its tray ready.

It reports "no-credential" until a provider key is in the Secret Service:

    secret-tool store --label='Highwater: zai (default)' \
        xdg:schema io.github.kakadon000.Highwater.ProviderKey provider zai account default

Everything it knows is readable without the GUI:

    busctl --user call io.github.kakadon000.Highwater.Daemon /io/github/kakadon000/Highwater \
        io.github.kakadon000.Highwater.Daemon1 GetStatus
